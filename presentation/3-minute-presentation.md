# FloodRoute AI — 3-Minute Live Hackathon Presentation Speech

> **Target Duration**: Exactly 3 minutes (180 seconds)  
> **Tone**: Professional, clear, grounded, and empathetic. No exaggerated statistics or unsubstantiated claims.

---

### 0:00 – 0:20 | PROBLEM
*"Good morning, judges. During intense monsoon rains and sudden urban flooding, everyday commuters, delivery workers, and families face serious uncertainty. People need to quickly understand four basic things:
- What are the current local weather conditions?
- What is the estimated flood risk in their area?
- Where are the nearest emergency shelters and medical services?
- And what are the route conditions or flood hazards on their path home?

Today, this critical information is scattered across different apps, news channels, and social media, leaving people to make transit decisions in the dark."*

---

### 0:20 – 0:45 | SOLUTION
*"Introducing FloodRoute AI — an AI-powered flood intelligence and route decision-support platform.

FloodRoute AI brings together real-time weather forecasts, digital terrain elevation, hydrological proximity, and community ground reports into one intuitive interface. Our mission is simple: to help people navigate through extreme monsoon conditions by providing clear, explainable environmental context and identifying routes with lower modeled flood-risk exposure."*

---

### 0:45 – 1:15 | LIVE MAP
*[Action: Show Interactive Live Map, demonstrate India-wide coverage, search or select Velachery, Chennai]*

*"Let's look at our Live Map. The platform covers locations across India and allows users to search any city, locality, or landmark, or simply use their current location.

Here, we've selected Velachery in Chennai—an area known for localized waterlogging due to its saucer-shaped topography. 

At a glance, the map displays:
- The selected location with sub-meter coordinate precision
- Real-time weather telemetry from Open-Meteo
- A clean, color-coded flood risk overlay
- Active weather bulletins and nearby emergency shelters

Everything is accessible without technical jargon, designed for quick comprehension during an emergency."*

---

### 1:15 – 1:45 | FLOOD RISK
*[Action: Open the Risk Analysis Card / Demo Step showing 78/100 HIGH RISK]*

*"When we examine the flood risk for Velachery, the system estimates a risk score of 78 out of 100, placing it in the HIGH risk category.

Rather than giving a black-box number, our model explains exactly why:
- First, heavy localized rainfall: 34 mm per hour precipitation based on forecast models.
- Second, terrain elevation: the area sits at just 4.2 meters above mean sea level.
- Third, drainage capacity: local stormwater runoff channels are operating near peak capacity.

Crucially: **The flood-risk result is a model estimate and is not an official government warning.** It is designed for situational awareness and advisory decision support, helping citizens take timely precautions."*

---

### 1:45 – 2:15 | ROUTE + EMERGENCY SERVICES
*[Action: Switch to Route Planner, show route from Velachery to Chennai Central, highlight comparative cards]*

*"Next, when a user needs to travel or evacuate, our flood-aware routing engine evaluates candidate paths.

Here, traveling from Velachery to Chennai Central:
- The direct route through Mount Road is 18.0 km and takes 20 minutes, but passes low-elevation basins with severe water ingress hazards.
- The alternative OMR Elevated Bypass is 19.4 km and takes 26 minutes, but traverses elevated flyovers with significantly lower modeled flood-risk exposure.

The platform also highlights nearby verified high-ground relief centers—like the Guru Nanak College shelter 1.2 kilometers away, with direct one-touch calling to India's 112 emergency helpline.

We want to emphasize: **the system supports transit decisions rather than guaranteeing road safety.** Citizens must always prioritize official directives from local disaster authorities and traffic police."*
