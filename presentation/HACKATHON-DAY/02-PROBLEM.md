# FloodRoute AI Platform — The Problem

## Why Urban Flooding Creates Deadly Transit Choke Points

During heavy monsoon downpours and convective cloudbursts across Indian urban centers (such as Chennai, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Patna), arterial streets turn into impassable torrents within 15 to 30 minutes.

### 1. Inability to Track Localized Increasing Risk
- Weather apps report broad citywide precipitation totals (e.g., "50 mm rain today") without resolving micro-catchment waterlogging or low-lying basin depressions.
- Citizens cannot determine where road risk is escalating until their vehicle is already submerged.

### 2. Blindness of Conventional Navigation
- Mainstream navigation platforms (Google Maps, Waze) optimize almost exclusively for traffic density and vehicle velocity.
- A road displaying clear green traffic can actually be submerged under three to four feet of standing stormwater in underpasses, causing stalled engines, drowned vehicles, and trapped ambulances.

### 3. Disconnected Emergency Response & Shelters
- High-ground evacuation shelters, NDRF/SDRF rescue boat stations, and operating hospitals are scattered across fragmented PDF circulars and social media posts, leaving vulnerable families stranded without immediate actionable guidance.
