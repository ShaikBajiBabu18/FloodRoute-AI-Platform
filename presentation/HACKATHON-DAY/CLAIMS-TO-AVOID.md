# FloodRoute AI Platform — Claims to Avoid & Accurate Language Guide

**Purpose:** Strict ethical presentation guidelines. Presenters must strictly avoid exaggerated or legally unverified claims during judging.

---

## Claims to Strictly Avoid vs. Accurate Language to Use

| ❌ DO NOT SAY (Misleading / Exaggerated) | ✅ USE INSTEAD (Accurate & Responsible) |
|---|---|
| *"100% accurate flood prediction"* | **"Model-estimated flood risk based on available inputs"** |
| *"Guaranteed safe route / 100% safe route"* | **"Lower modeled flood-risk exposure"** |
| *"Official statutory flood warning"* | **"Advisory decision-support estimate"** |
| *"Government-certified / government-approved"* | **"Aligned with NDMA guidelines and public weather telemetry"** |
| *"Predicts every flash flood everywhere"* | **"Correlates precipitation intensity and 30m elevation contours"** |
| *"Works perfectly with zero errors"* | **"Resilient architecture with automated offline fallbacks"** |
| *"Real-time everywhere across India"* | **"Ingests open meteorological sensor grids across India"** |
| *"Official government live alerts"* | **"Disaster bulletins [clearly labeled as DEMO DATA where simulated]"** |

---

## Core Disclaimers to Emphasize

1. **Advisory Decision Support:**  
   *"FloodRoute AI is an intelligent decision-support tool designed to provide visibility during emergencies; it is not a statutory government agency and does not issue legal disaster decrees."*

2. **Rapidly Changing Monsoon Conditions:**  
   *"Monsoon conditions and water accumulation can change rapidly; commuters must always follow on-ground instructions from local traffic police and disaster management authorities."*

3. **Topographical Resolution:**  
   *"Our model incorporates 30-meter SRTM digital elevation data, which accurately resolves basin contours but does not detect sub-meter street features like elevated curbs or localized curb drainage blockages."*
