*(Show Home Page Dashboard at `http://localhost:8080/`)*

> "Judges, during intense monsoon downpours across India, citizens and emergency responders face dangerous decisions: Where is flood risk escalating right now? Which roads are becoming impassable? And where can vulnerable families find accessible emergency help?
>

### [00:20 – 00:45] THE SOLUTION: Introducing FloodRoute AI
*(Highlight Dashboard Metrics & Tagline)*

>

We never claim guaranteed safety, but we give drivers the data to avoid submerged choke points.
