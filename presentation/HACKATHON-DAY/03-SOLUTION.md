# FloodRoute AI Platform — The Solution

## A Decision-Support Ecosystem for Urban Flood Resilience

FloodRoute AI bridges the gap between atmospheric weather telemetry, digital elevation models, and vehicular routing.

### 1. Nationwide Geospatial Intelligence
- A responsive, high-performance GIS map covering all 36 Indian states and union territories.
- Live overlays for precipitation intensity, active hazard markers, and municipal relief shelters.

### 2. Transparent Multi-Factor Risk Engine
- Calculates an explainable 0–100 flood-risk score correlating rainfall rate, topographic ground elevation, drainage saturation, active disaster bulletins, and verified citizen reports.
- Explains exactly why a location received its score rather than presenting an uninterpretable black-box percentage.

### 3. Route Decision Support (Lower Modeled Risk Exposure)
- Analyzes candidate trajectories against hazard polygons.
- Compares shortest direct corridors with elevated highway bypasses, guiding users toward routes with **lower modeled flood-risk exposure**.

### 4. Immediate Life-Safety Discovery
- Direct one-tap 112 national emergency dialing.
- Categorized discovery of nearest high-ground shelters, police outposts, and hospitals.

### 5. Context-Aware AI Copilot & Offline SMS Fallback
- Natural-language assistant translating complex weather and elevation data into clear pre-travel safety advice.
- Low-bandwidth SMS command parsing (`FLOOD [CITY]`, `ROUTE [A] TO [B]`) for zero-internet disaster zones.
