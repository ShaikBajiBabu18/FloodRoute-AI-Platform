# FloodRoute AI — Pitch Scripts

> **Official Project Description**:  
> *"FloodRoute AI is an AI-powered flood intelligence and route decision-support platform that combines weather data, location intelligence, flood-risk analysis, emergency services and route analysis in one interactive platform."*

---

## 1. 30-Second Elevator Pitch
> *"Every monsoon across India, sudden cloudbursts trap commuters in submerged underpasses and blind spots. Traditional weather apps report rain totals, but they cannot tell you if your road is passable.*
>
> ***FloodRoute AI** is an AI-powered flood intelligence and route decision-support platform that combines weather data, location intelligence, flood-risk analysis, emergency services and route analysis in one interactive platform.
>
> By cross-referencing live rainfall, digital elevation contours, and verified community reports, we guide citizens along routes with lower modeled flood-risk exposure and connect them instantly to high-ground emergency shelters."*

---

## 2. 60-Second Hackathon Semi-Final Pitch
> *"Judges, when heavy rain hits Mumbai, Chennai, or Bengaluru, the tragedy isn't just the downpour—it's the lack of actionable transit intelligence. Standard navigation tools blindly route ambulances and families directly into inundated low-lying basins.
>
> To solve this, we created **FloodRoute AI**—an AI-powered flood intelligence and route decision-support platform that combines weather data, location intelligence, flood-risk analysis, emergency services and route analysis in one interactive platform.
>
> Unlike black-box models, our risk engine provides fully explainable scoring based on rainfall intensity, terrain elevation, stormwater drainage capacity, and river proximity. Our flood-aware routing engine computes transit corridors specifically optimized for lower modeled flood-risk exposure, while our vision microservice verifies citizen waterlogging photos on the ground.
>
> With a real-time citizen portal, offline-resilient caching, and an Incident Command Center for disaster authorities, FloodRoute AI bridges the gap between atmospheric forecasts and ground-level survival."*

## 3. 3-Minute Grand Finale Stage Pitch

### Act 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)
*"Good morning, esteemed judges and audience. In December 2015 in Chennai, and repeatedly during Mumbai monsoons, lives were lost not because help didn't exist, but because people drove into flooded underpasses without warning.
A standard weather app tells you: '35 mm of rain is falling.' But can your hatchback cross the road? Where is the nearest dry hospital? Which flyover avoids the overflowing canal? Standard navigation apps have zero awareness of rising water levels."*

### Act 2: The Solution & Architecture (0:45 – 1:45)
*"This is why we built **FloodRoute AI**—an AI-powered flood intelligence and route decision-support platform that combines weather data, location intelligence, flood-risk analysis, emergency services and route analysis in one interactive platform.
Our platform unites four powerful systems:
1. **Live GIS & Hydrometeorological Intelligence**: Ingesting real-time Open-Meteo precipitation, digital elevation models, and river telemetry.
2. **Explainable AI Risk Engine**: A deterministic multi-factor model evaluating rain rate, basin elevation, drainage capacity, and river proximity with 100% factor attribution transparency.
3. **Flood-Aware Routing Engine**: Computing transit corridors optimized for lower modeled flood-risk exposure by penalizing submerged road links.
4. **Emergency Services & Incident Command Center**: Connecting stranded citizens to verified high-ground relief shelters, while giving district magistrates a live situational command deck."*

### Act 3: Live Impact & Vision (1:45 – 3:00)
*"During a crisis, speed and clarity save lives. When a citizen opens FloodRoute AI, they don't see a confusing spreadsheet—they see a clean, accessible interface with high-contrast safety indicators, one-tap route guidance to high ground, and a direct line to the National 112 emergency network.
If internet fails, our offline service-worker caching and pre-computed topological fallbacks ensure critical evacuation data is never lost.
FloodRoute AI transforms raw weather data into life-saving transit decisions.
Thank you."*
