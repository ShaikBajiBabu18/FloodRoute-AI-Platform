# FloodRoute AI — Quick Demo Card

### START
Open dashboard at `http://localhost:8080/`.

### SHOW
Click **Live Map** to display nationwide India GIS coverage.

### SEARCH
Type `Patna` (or click `Chennai`) to fly to the municipal basin.

### ANALYZE
Open Risk Analysis to generate the 0–100 flood-risk score.

### EXPLAIN
Show the transparent breakdown table: rainfall intensity, basin elevation, and citizen reports.

### ROUTE
Open Route Planner; calculate directions and highlight the green Elevated Bypass with **lower modeled flood-risk exposure**.

### EMERGENCY
Open Emergency portal; show one-tap 112 dialing and nearest high-ground relief shelters.

### AI
Open Copilot Chat; click *"Why is the risk elevated?"* to demonstrate context-aware plain-language guidance.

### CLOSE
*"FloodRoute AI bridges weather telemetry and navigation to protect lives and vehicles during severe urban flooding across India."*
