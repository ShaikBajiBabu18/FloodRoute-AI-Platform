# FloodRoute AI Platform — 60-Second Spoken Explanation

**Prompt:** *"You have one minute. Explain what you built."*  
**Duration:** Exactly 60 Seconds (155 Words)  
**Tone:** Candid, engineering-focused, zero marketing fluff.

---

> "During severe monsoon downpours across India, standard navigation apps only optimize for traffic speed. An empty road can be submerged under three feet of water in an underpass, and drivers have no way of knowing until their engine stalls.
> 
> We built **FloodRoute AI** to bridge that gap. 
> 
> It's a full-stack platform that pulls real-time rainfall from Open-Meteo, cross-references it with 30-meter NASA digital elevation models, and calculates an explainable 0 to 100 flood-risk score. 
> 
> When a user plans a trip, our routing service samples the road waypoints and flags submerged basins, actively recommending elevated arterial bypasses with **lower modeled flood-risk exposure**. 
> 
> We also built in one-tap 112 calling, shelter routing, an offline conversational AI Copilot, and an admin console with computer-vision flood depth analysis. 
> 
> It's an intelligent decision-support tool, not an official emergency decree, and it has automated offline fallbacks if external APIs fail."
